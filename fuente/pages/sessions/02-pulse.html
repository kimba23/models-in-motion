2 · Pulse: slowing down

Oct 30. Thirteen takes become one shot when you decide when each one shows. That decision is a rhythm.

This field moves so fast that its forms die before anyone understands them. Tools from four years ago no longer exist. A pulse is the opposite of that speed: something that comes back, and so can be studied. Today we slow down on purpose: thirteen takes of one shot, six seconds, looked at frame by frame.

A short history of making images move

2021–22Disco Diffusion, a Colab notebook. Its v5 (Feb 2022) added 3D animation: every image is born from the one before it, pushed by a camera you write.
2022Deforum takes that to Stable Diffusion. A new way of animating: not frames drawn, not footage shot, but an image that feeds on itself while numbers move the camera. Hybrid video mixes a real video into every frame.
2022WarpFusion, by Sxela (Alex Spirin): diffusion painted over real video, each frame warped along the movement of the one before, so the style sticks to the motion.
2022–23Parseq, by Robin Fernandes (rewbs): every value of a Deforum animation (zoom, rotation, strength, seed, the prompt) becomes a curve in time, written in frames, seconds or beats, locked to the music.
2023AnimateDiff: a motion module trained on video, plugged into Stable Diffusion. The model itself learns to move.
nowVideo models (LTX, Wan, Sora...) replaced all of it in about two years.

Watch: Parseq tutorial, part 3: audio sync by rewbs. The explanation and the image are on screen at the same time: the teaching is the work. (The whole series.)

What survives the tools: time is a variable you write (frame, second, beat) · the music is a curve · the image follows; it does not illustrate.

They had one take and an optical printer. We have thirteen takes of the same moment, and a script.

Arc and pulse

A Fibonacci pattern (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21...) never repeats: it reads as a gesture, a startle that settles. A tresillo (x..x..x., 3-3-2) repeats: it reads as a groove.

Godfried Toussaint, The Euclidean Algorithm Generates Traditional Musical Rhythms (2005, PDF): spread k hits as evenly as possible over n steps with Euclid's algorithm, and out come rhythms from all over the world. 3 in 8 is the tresillo, 5 in 8 the cinquillo, 7 in 12 a West African bell pattern.

The dose

Cut every shot and the cut stops reading as a decision; it reads as a filter. Nothing rests, so nothing lands. Establish the shot, make it alive, then break it a little.

In the lab

  1. Update the tools (Install, step 2).
  2. Queue 13 takes of one shot from your page: workflow 03, type 13 next to Run, press once.
  3. Move the 13 takes into a folder of their own. Copy RUN.bat and pulse_cut.py (both are in the kit's tools folder) into it.
  4. Your page's rhythm. Read one line of your page aloud. Write it as x where it leans and . where it rests. Put that string in RHYTHM at the top of pulse_cut.py. Save. Double-click RUN.
    Example, a line of Whitman (1855): I celebrate myself, and sing myself → .x.x.x.x.x
  5. Try the dose: WINDOW = (2, 4) cuts only between seconds 2 and 4.
  6. A script of your own. With a chat, from an idea of yours: how. RUN.
